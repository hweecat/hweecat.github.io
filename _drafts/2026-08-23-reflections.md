It's been a long while (3+ years) since I've written a long-form reflection post.

Not because I have not been reflecting on my life, but because the thoughts weigh too heavily on me to pen down and admit that I even have these thoughts in a platform that is supposed to showcase myself.

The version of the reflections I shared on LinkedIn? Self-censored, filtered and polished for the professional audience - the recruiters, hiring managers, and people I've connected with from events, educational years and community work.

I've shared snippets about my disillusionment with my career trajectory, the grief I've been holding on from my past experiences, and the lessons I've learned along the way. Having a space that is separate from the expectations of a professional platform allows me to be more honest with my thoughts without the pressure of maintaining a perfect image that is suitable for professional consumption.

Where do I begin?
